Our Story

LUMÉA was born from a desire to return to the essentials. In a world of over-complicated routines and harsh chemicals, we sought a path that honored both traditional botanical wisdom and modern dermatological science.

Founded by herbalist Elena Vance and biochemist Dr. Marcus Thorne, LUMÉA represents the perfect synergy between the garden and the laboratory.
